A1. What Saathi is

An extended, out-of-hours support and continuity service. It triages caregiver concerns into a teleconsultation call-back; it does not give treatment by chat.

ApproveChange

A2. Check in to Saathi from the hospital record

At the end of an OPD visit, the doctor clicks "Check in to Saathi" in the hospital system. Saathi receives the patient's details, prescription, notes and follow-up plan (for example a phone follow-up in 2 weeks and an OPD visit in 1 month). For the demo, a dummy hospital page will hold one synthetic case that you write.

ApproveChange

A3. Reminders on WhatsApp

Morning and evening medicine reminders listed by medicine, refill reminders, OPD appointment reminders and phone follow-up reminders. Families tap "Given" or "Not given", which builds the medicine-compliance chart.

ApproveChange

A4. What "Acknowledge" means

The on-call doctor acknowledging a report means it is accepted into the call-back list. It does not close the report. The report closes only after the call, when the doctor writes call notes and the advice given.

A6. Three kinds of login

(1) Doctors, nurses and psychologists see full medical details. (2) Non-medical staff (call manager) see only date-wise counts, urgency, status, patient name and hospital ID, and can export a daily PDF report. (3) Patients and families sign in with a one-time code by phone number or email.

ApproveChange

A7. Fees (optional per organisation)

Paid services can ask for a call-back fee when the family requests a call (audio or video), with follow-up pricing (for example the first follow-up within 15 days at half fee). Free services such as NGOs switch fees off. The admin sees a "fee paid" checklist. No payment is collected in the demo.

ApproveChange

A8. Prescriptions after a teleconsult

After the call the doctor may write a prescription with a digital signature, sent to the family on WhatsApp. Following the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines, Saathi blocks Schedule X, narcotic and psychotropic drugs and anti-cancer drugs on teleconsult prescriptions. Records are kept for 3 years under the DPDP Act and IT rules.

ApproveChange

A9. Demo language and channel

English only for the demo. WhatsApp and a web page for families. Telegram is not needed.

B5. C6 Constipation while taking morphine

1. Morphine almost always causes constipation. 2. Give the laxative every day, even if the patient has passed stool. 3. Offer fluids often. 4. Gentle movement helps. 5. Tell the team if there is no stool for 3 days, or if there is belly pain or vomiting.

B6. C12 Counting breaths and checking drowsiness

Counting breaths: wait until the patient is resting quietly. Watch or feel the chest or tummy rise. Count each rise for one full minute by the phone timer. • 12 or fewer → RED: the doctor is alerted. • 13–15 → the team reviews today (pending your decision, see D2). Checking drowsiness: call their name and talk to them. If they keep falling asleep while talking or eating, or are hard to wake, the doctor is alerted immediately.
